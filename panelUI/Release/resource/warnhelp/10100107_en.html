SuckBack End Error


Description：
1. The suck-back movement does not reach the required end position and takes longer than the suck-back alarm time.


Troubleshooting：
1.Adjust the suck-back end position;
Note: Check the reading of the electric scale, which should be equal to or larger than the set suck-back end position；


2.Check if the nozzle is blocked and the material in the barrel is normal.
