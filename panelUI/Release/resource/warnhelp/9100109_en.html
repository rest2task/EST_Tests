Press Have Not Set


Description：
1.During the self-tuning process of the servo's proportional valve, the set pressure is not reached in 5 seconds.


Troubleshooting：
1.If multiple DA values become 4000, check any leakage in the oil circuit;


2.Check if the proportional valve is adjusted properly;
Adjustment method: After the machine is assembled, use an internal hexagonal wrench to tighten the screw clockwise at the end of the valve; Test the pressure of the machine. After the pressure becomes normal, loosen the same screw and parallel it with the tail cover.


3.Check if the overflow valve is well tightened.
