Temperature Error


Description：
1.One or more sections of the tube exceed the set temperature limit during the charge, injection, purge, pressurization or suck-back.


Troubleshooting：
1.Switch on the motor and check the fuse;


2.Heat the tube to the set temperature;


3.If the temperature fails to rise in a long period of time, check "Heating (1-6)" outputs on the IO interface


Note: Enter the heating output interface. Move the cursor to relevant outputs. Press ENTER to start compulsory output. Switch the multi-meter to voltage and check the output (DC24V or AC22V);

Check if the heating ring or cable is broken.
