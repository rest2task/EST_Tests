1.Motor On Hold Time: It is the protection duration after the motor completes Y->△ switch. During the period, the motor remains still;


2.Motor Y Start: It is the duration of the motor's Y starting;


3.Motor Delta Time: It is the time that the Y->△ switch process takes;


4.Motor Count: When the number is not 1, output terminals of the motors do not output;


5.Motor On Alarm Time: When the input terminal "Motor On Completes" receives no input within the time, the alarm "Motor On Fails" will be made;
