window name：IO Out1

1.Function and Status:

1.1 The function of each output is described on the right side of the number and its status is indicated by the dot on the left. When the dot turns green, the output is effective.


1.2 Where the hardware of an output is broken, you can replace the output with a spare output in "IO Redefine". Notably, you need to reconnect the IO terminal on the PLC accordingly.


2.The host of A620 has 24 outputs, while that of A920 has 32 outputs.


3.Press ENTER in manual status. The chosen output will output compulsorily.
