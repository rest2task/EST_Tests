Window Name：clamp


1.The electric scale shows the minimum value when the clamp is fully closed and the maximum value when it is fully opened；


2.Inputs of the end position are limited by its neighboring positions. Therefore, inputs of the clamp closing process must be set from Close #1 and those of the clamp opening process from Open End；


3.Clamp opening and closing: close #1 is output when the mold’s position is larger than 350; Close #2 is output when the mold’s position is between 300 and 350; (Open #1 is output when the mold’s position is smaller than 40; Open #2 is output when the mold’s position is between 40 and 80); All the stages are output in the order.


4.Close #3 and Open #4: They can be enabled or disabled in PAR1 and PAR2。
