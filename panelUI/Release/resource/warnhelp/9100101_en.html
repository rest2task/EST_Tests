Pressure Sensor Invalid


Description：
1.The system gives a pressure sensor failure alarm (the pressure sensor is 1-5V).


Troubleshooting：
1.Ensure that the output voltage of the pressure sensor ranges between 1 and 5V;


2.When the machine is idle, the feedback voltage of the pressure sensor should be larger than 5/7, approximately 0. 71V;
Note: Check the pressure sensor signal's voltage to ground as shown in the following figure, which should be about 1V.

3.Check the plug and cable of the sensor. Replace the pressure sensor.
