window name：Warn Para

1.Auto Alarm On Cycle Number:

1.1When the value is 0 or auto alarm not used, auto alarm is disabled and alarm parameters are set to values on this page;

1.2When it is not 0 and auto alarm used, auto alarm will be enabled when the preset cycle number is reached;；


2.Closing Low Pressure Alarm Time: maximum time of the clamp closing process in low pressure before alarm;


3.Closing High Pressure Alarm Time: maximum time of the clamp closing process in high pressure before alarm;


4.Charging Alarm Time: It is the total time of the charging process. The alarm "Fail to Complete Charging in Time" will be made when the time expires;


5.Suck Back Alarm Time: It is the total time of the suck-back process. The alarm "Fail to Suck Back" will be made when the time expires;


6.Max and Min Alarming Tube Temp: When the difference between the actual temperature of the charging tube and the preset temperature is larger than the maximum or minimum value, the system prompts "Temperature Error";


7.Max Alarming Clamp Opening/Closing Time: When CODE0 includes 40 in configuration and clamp opening/closing alarm is enabled, the alarm "Clamp Opening Failure" or "Clamp Closing Failure" will be made when the clamp opening or closing process is not completed within the preset time;


8."Heater Off Time" and "Buzzer Count": When the number of times that the buzzer flickers reaches the preset value that triggers the alarm, the motor will be turned off and the heater will be turned off too after the "Heater Off Time" expires;
