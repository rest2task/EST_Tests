Mold Adjustment：
1. Slow Mold ADJ: When you manually adjust the mold, the mold is adjusted in a low speed in the beginning. If the value is 0, the mold is adjusted in a high speed from the beginning.


2. Mold ADJ Count: counting value of the first adjustment in the automatic mold adjustment mode;


3. ADJ Proportion: It is adjusted by 0.01mm. It is set to 5 generally, that means 0.05mm.
