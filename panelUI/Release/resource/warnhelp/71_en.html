window name：Other Function

1. Power Door: When the power door is used,

1.1 Add 80 to Code4 to enable this option;

1.2 Add 200 to Code3 when a hydraulic pressure power door is used.

1.3 Two spare output points must be defined, if there is no power door switch output point on the IO page.

1.4 Install the On and Off buttons of the power door. Weld a group of always-on contacts of the power door’s On and Off buttons on the connection points OPEN and CLOSE on the rear of the panel.

1.5 Continuous Mode: Press the button and the switch of the power door will receive continuous output before the power door is fully open or closed. Add 4000 to Code0 to enable the function. Otherwise, press and hold the button to get continuous output.


2.Power Door Opening Mode:

1) Before clamp opening: The power door opens when the clamp starts opening and ejection is performed after the door is fully open.

2) After clamp opening: Ejection is performed and the door opens concurrently after the clamp is fully open.


Power Door Opening Time: It is used to protect the door. If the door fails to fully open before the time expires, the door opening output will be stopped. Set the value to 0 to disable the function.
