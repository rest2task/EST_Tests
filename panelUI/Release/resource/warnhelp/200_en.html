window name：Machine AD Adjust

1.Reset: When the screw, mold, ejector and pressure sensor are reset (clamp is fully closed, full injection, ejector returns completely, and system pressure is 0), and the current position is not reset, choose Reset and press ENT to reset the position.


2.ADJ Pos: When the screw, mold and ejector are at the maximum position (clamp is fully open, suck-back completely, fully ejected), measure the distance between the mechanism and the reset position. Enter the value into the corresponding 'ADJ Pos'. The adjustment is completed when the current position reaches the measured value.


3.Deduct 2-5 mm from the maximum mechanical stroke of each movement and enter the value into the mechanical stroke column on the machine configuration page. This limits the end position of each movement and prevents mechanical parts from colliding with each other.
