ChkMdAuto Pos Error


Description：
1.No falling finished product is detected by the electric eye. (The detection is only performed when auto electric eye is enabled)


Troubleshooting：
1.Check if products fall across the detection area and if they can be detected by the electric eye;


2.Check the electric eye input on the IO interface and if the electric eye is damaged.
Note: See page 3 for the checking method.
