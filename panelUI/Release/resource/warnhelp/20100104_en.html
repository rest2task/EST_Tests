Mold Open Time Out


Description：
1.The clamp opening time exceeds the maximum alarming clamp opening time during the opening process;


2.It happens in automatic Chenhsong mold adjustment. The clamp opening end position exceeds the effective area;


3.The mold is not fully open before ejection.


Troubleshooting：
1.Solve the mechanical failure that causes the clamp opening delay. Reset Max Alarming Clamp Opening Time;


2.Check input Clamp Open End on the IO interface and if the stroke switch of Clamp Open End is damaged；
Note: See page 3 for the checking method;


3.Set the effective clamp opening area properly;
Note: |Current actual position - end opening position| < the effective opening area;


4.Adjust clamp opening pressure and flow parameters.
