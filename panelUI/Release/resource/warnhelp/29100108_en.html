Door Check Error


Description：
1.Input Hydraulic Safety Check, Hydraulic Safety Check 2 or Protective Electric Eye is triggered in the clamp closing process.


Troubleshooting：
1.Enter I/O interface to check input Hydraulic Safety Check, Hydraulic Safety Check 2 and Protective Electric Eye, and if the related switches are damaged;


2.Check input Hydraulic Safety Check, Hydraulic Safety Check 2 and Protective Electric Eye or input signals.
