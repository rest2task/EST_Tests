window name：Machine DA Adjust

1.DA Linear ADJ: When the machine is an ordinary machine, you need to perform pressure and flow DC adjustment


2.DA Selection: There are three groups. The first group corresponds with channel P and F. By default, it is the system pressure and flow; The second group corresponds with port Pre2 and is generally the charge back pressure; The third and fourth group corresponds with the expansion board's DA channel.


3.Start the motor and choose DA pressure adjustment:

3.1Set the start pressure, generally 30%.

3.2Reset the pressure. Choose ADJ and press ENT. After it becomes In ADJ, press up and down arrows on the panel to reset the pressure or corresponding current meter.

3.3The step of adjusting the maximum pressure is same as the fore-mentioned one. Adjust it until the pressure meter stays at the maximum pressure of the system.

3.4The first method of linear adjustment is to enter the pressure shown on the pressure meter into the corresponding pressure Bar column. The second method is to change the input until the pressure meter's reading is same as the Bar value. Press ENT after completing adjustment.


4.Choose DA flow adjustment: The procedure is same as step 3. You need to calculate the flow by measuring the screw's rotate speed. Therefore, you need to select “Output Charge In Flow ADJ” on the left side; Set and enable heating. Flow can be adjusted only when the barrel reaches about 200 centigrade
