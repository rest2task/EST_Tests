Pressure Sensor Not Zero


Description：
1.When the motor is started, the feedback pressure of the pressure sensor is lower than -2Bar or larger than 5Bar.


Troubleshooting：
1.Do not switch on the motor when there is residual pressure. Press Nozzle RET. to open the operation valve and release residual pressure.


2.After the actual pressure falls to 0, enter the electric scale adjustment page to reset the sensor.
