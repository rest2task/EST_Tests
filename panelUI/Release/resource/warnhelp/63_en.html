window name：Temp Para2

1.Cooling On Temp：
IMM starts cooling when the oil temperature exceeds the value.


2.Cooling Off Temp：
IMM stops cooling when the oil temperature is lower than the value.


3.Tube Fan On Difference：The cooling fan is turned on when the difference between the charge tube’s temperature and set temperature is reached.


4.Tube Fan Off Difference: The cooling fan is turned off when the difference between the charge tube’s temperature and set temperature is reached.
