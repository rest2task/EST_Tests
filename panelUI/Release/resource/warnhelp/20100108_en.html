Opn 1-Slow Posn Error


Description:
1.In the first clamp closing cycle under automatic mold adjustment, the closing time exceeds Auto ADJ Low Pressure Time.


Troubleshooting:
1.Check if the electric scale of the clamp is null when the clamp is fully closed and crank arm stretches straight. If not, null the scale;
Note: Make sure that the actual reading of the electric scale of clamp opening and closing +10 < end position of Open #1.


2.Increase the Auto ADJ Low Pressure Time.
