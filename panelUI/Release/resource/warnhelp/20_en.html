winodow name：Eject/Air Blow Setting

1.Delay: delay in actions by the pressure, flow and direction valves;


2.Air blow setting：

2.1There are four air blow modes: clamp opening time/position, injection time/position;

2.2Action time: It is the output time of the air blow valve；

2.3Delay: When the air blow mode is clamp opening/injection time, the IMM will blow after the delay upon clamp opening and injection；

2.4Action position: When the air blow mode is clamp opening/injection position, the IMM will only start blowing after the set position is reached.
