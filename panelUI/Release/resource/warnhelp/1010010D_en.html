Charge Low


Description:
1.It happens on a BMC IMM. Low ChargeDown Material Level or ChargeDown FWD End is detected.


Troubleshooting:
1.Check if the charge down cylinder reaches the forward end and the material level reaches the bottom limit;


2.Enter I/O enter to check input Low ChargeDown Material Level and ChargeDown FWD End, and if the detection switch is damaged.
Note: See page 3 for the checking method.
